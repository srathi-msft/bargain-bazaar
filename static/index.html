How it works

  1. You get ₹9,200 and a shopping list of 6 things. Sticker prices add up to more than you have.
  2. Tap an item and haggle with Raju Anna. Offer a price and a reason, like "I saw the backpack on Amazon for 1,500, can you do 1,450?". Happy with his price? Press Buy. Typing "deal" never buys by accident.
  3. Raju Anna is tough and every shopper meets a different Anna. Be friendly and specific and he softens; be rude, pushy or lowball and he digs in, and may even put the price up.
  4. Fill your bag for the lowest total to win. A full bag always beats a half-full one. Tie? The earlier finisher wins. Joining late is fine: the clock is shared and your wallet is full.

Quick tips

  • Offer a number: "I'll pay 1,400" or "make it 1,350". Numbers in your own script work too.
  • Give him a reason, like a cheaper price you saw elsewhere (say the price), a flaw you spotted, buying more than one thing, a good joke. Specific beats generic, and the same reason twice works less.
  • He is a real shopkeeper with moods: be friendly and he softens; shout, bully or lowball and he digs in, and he may even put the price up. Watch his mood face and the patience dots.
  • Walk away can work once per item, but only after real haggling, and he may just let you go.
  • Flattery, begging and tricks never work on a man with 30 years of experience.
  • 🔊 Raju Anna makes little sounds (a hmm, a tsk, a cash register). Not for you? Tap the 🔊 at the top to switch them off.
